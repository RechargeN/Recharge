## 1. Purpose

This queue states what happens after the internal Product-owner roll-up. It
prevents another planning cycle and makes clear that accepting Batch F does not
complete the real specialist, independent and qualified reviews.

## 2. Queue rules

- Real reviewer identity and authority are recorded only from attributable
  instructions.
- Work may run in parallel only when it uses the same frozen target.
- Findings are integrated before dependent decisions.
- Qualified/legal lanes are never replaced by Product-owner acceptance.
- Runtime evidence waits for its authorized gate.

## 4. Parallelization lanes

P1 items may run concurrently. BCK-05 reviews may begin with P1 because their
pre-G1 design questions do not require cloud evidence. X2/X3 may overlap only
when the target and findings visibility rules in the assignment manifest are
satisfied.

Per-source eligibility, OD promotion, parent Approval, D1 and G1 remain
sequential.

## 5. Evidence packet per queue item

Every result includes reviewer identity/role, authority/qualification basis,
independence/conflict disclosure, exact target/hash, scope, findings, verdict,
retained gates, date/timezone and linkable evidence. Missing fields are
Inconclusive.

## 6. Stop conditions

Stop the affected lane when:

- the target hash changes;
- reviewer authority/qualification cannot be evidenced;
- a Critical/High contradiction is found;
- source terms prohibit or ambiguously permit intended use;
- requested evidence would expose credentials/personal data;
- work would require unauthorized network/runtime/cloud mutation;
- another owner decision is required first.

## 7. Explicitly later executable evidence

The queue does not pull forward project provisioning, IAM bindings, telemetry,
cost reconciliation, restore/tabletop/worker tests, parser/ingestion, mobile
adapters, deployment or activation. Those remain at named post-G1/R-stage or
pre-traffic gates.

## 8. Progress reporting

Report counts by completed mandatory row, never one blended percentage. A
single unresolved mandatory blocker keeps its parent/stage blocked regardless
of how many optional rows are complete.
